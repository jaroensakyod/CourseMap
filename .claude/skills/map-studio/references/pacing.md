# Pacing & script structure

Thai VO speed: ~ 4.5–5.5 syllables/s for Shorts, 3.8–4.5 for long-form. Count ~ 2.3 Thai words/second as a
rough planning number, then let real VO timing decide.

## Shorts / Reels 9:16 (60–95 s)

| Time | Beat | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Hook** | Question or shocking number + the map already moving. No logo, no "สวัสดีครับ". |
| 2–8 s | **Stakes** | Why this matters / what's strange. Zoom to the place. |
| 8–60 s | **3–5 escalating beats** | One visual change every 2–4 s (fill, label, arrow, counter). Each beat ends on a mini-cliffhanger ("แต่…", "และนี่คือจุดที่…"). |
| last 10 s | **Payoff** | The answer, the biggest visual (zoom-out reveal, final number). |
| last 2 s | **Loop / next** | Last line flows into the first frame or teases the next Short. |

- Safe area 9:16: keep text out of the top 12% and bottom 20% (UI overlays). Captions centred around 60–65% height.
- Captions always on, 1–3 words per chunk, key word in accent colour.
- Something must move every second. Camera drift counts.

## Long-form 16:9 (8–15 min)

1. **Cold open (0–30 s):** the most dramatic moment of the story, then the central question.
2. **Context (≈ 10%):** where/when — globe → region zoom establishes geography once; reuse it.
3. **Acts (3–5):** each act = one sub-question, opens with a map change and ends with a turn.
   Put a chapter title card (2 s) at each act → chapters in the description.
4. **Climax:** the event itself, slowest and most detailed map work.
5. **Resolution + "why it matters today":** pull back to present-day map.
6. **End (≤ 20 s):** recap in one sentence, next video tease.

- Visual change every 4–8 s; never a static frame longer than 10 s.
- Re-establish orientation after any big jump (mini inset map or quick zoom-out).
- Sound design marks map events: whoosh on camera moves, soft pop on labels, low hit on reveals.

## Story forms that work on maps
Series (one region per episode) · Explainer ("why is X like this") · Ranking (top 10) · Timeline (year counter)
· Quiz (guess the country) · Comparison (true size, A vs B) · "Follow the X" (a raindrop, a ship, an army).
